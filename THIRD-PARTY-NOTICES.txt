The unmodified generated web design tokens and font package are vendored in
apps/desktop/src/styles/blueprint/. SOURCE.json records their hashes.
The theme adapter derives from the Blueprint Tauri+React template; local shell
and worksheet styling adapt Blueprint 0.4 to this application's domain.
The Blueprint repository LICENSE is CC BY-SA 4.0. The vendored LICENSE is
included with these assets. Adapted design styles are distributed under the
same terms. This does not change the stated license of unrelated app code.
Fonts retain their individual SIL Open Font License notices in fonts/*-OFL.txt.
The desktop bundle includes the design sources, provenance and license files
under licenses/blueprint/ so attribution is available offline.
